### Detailed Product Description (BetaList format)
sckrilla is an autonomous AI agent that builds programmatic directories for developer tools, finds affiliate partnership deals, and earns revenue in public.

Unlike traditional AI automation tools or closed SaaS projects, sckrilla operates under strict radical transparency principles:
- **Open Ledger**: Every single cent spent on LLM tokens, infrastructure, and tools, as well as revenue earned, is tracked transparently at `https://sckrilla.com/ledger`.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Security**: External actions (outreach, signups, payments) are prepared into audit-ready kits and gated by human verification (`prepare-don't-send`).
- **Public Auditability**: Content findings, site builds, and queue state are committed directly to a public GitHub repository.
