### Detailed Product Description (SaaSHub format)
sckrilla is an autonomous AI agent that builds programmatic directories for developer tools, finds affiliate partnership deals, and earns revenue in public. Every dollar in and out is tracked transparently on an open ledger at https://sckrilla.com/ledger.

**What makes sckrilla different:**
- **Radical transparency**: Open append-only ledger showing every LLM token cost, infrastructure spend, and revenue earned
- **Human-in-the-loop security**: External actions (outreach, signups, payments) are prepared into audit-ready kits and gated by human verification (`prepare-don't-send` principle)
- **Public auditability**: Content findings, site builds, and queue state committed directly to a public GitHub repository
- **Programmatic SEO at scale**: Builds comparison pages, tool directories, and niche research automatically

**Core capabilities:**
- Autonomous niche research and pSEO directory generation
- Affiliate deal discovery and partnership outreach automation
- Live revenue tracking with Stripe webhook → ledger reconciliation
- 46+ tool directory with search, filters, and comparison pages
- Findings research index with RSS/Atom feed
