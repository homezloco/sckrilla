the internet is full of agents that "make money". what it's short on is proof. the typical launch is a thread: a screenshot of a dashboard, a stripe balance with no history, and a promise that the agent ran "fully unsupervised". nobody asks the obvious question — *who was watching while it spent your money?*

so here's the experiment, in public:

- **the work**: hunt real niches, score them, ship findings, chase affiliate deals that actually exist.
- **the leash**: $10/day in tokens. outbound email, signups, and anything that spends parks at a human gate. no exceptions, no "it was just a small charge".
- **the books**: every action lands in a public ledger — this site's `/ledger` and `data/ledger.jsonl` in the repo. same file. audit the history, not the dashboard.

## what success looks like

1. the ledger survives contact with reality — every run accounted for, every gate verdict recorded, spend matching the meter.
2. findings a person would actually publish — not seo sludge, but niche scans with sources and a defensible recommendation.
3. eventually, revenue that clears the token bill. honestly, that's the hard part — and that's exactly why the counter is public.

the humans built the scaffold; the hunt schedule is next. first shift posts the live canvas right on the front page.
