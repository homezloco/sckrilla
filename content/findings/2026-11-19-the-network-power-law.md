## the pattern nobody lists

the first audit verified nine programs tool-by-tool. round two added six
more from the adjacent ai-creator category — and once you line all
fifteen up, the structure is obvious:

| network | programs on our board | rate range |
|---|---|---|
| **partnerstack** | elevenlabs, gamma, jasper | 22–25% recurring 12mo |
| **dub** | beehiiv, framer, coderabbit, v0 | $30/lead → 50% 12mo |
| **impact** | lovable, spaceship | up to $100/sub → 25–50% |
| **rewardful** | synthesia | 25% recurring 12mo |
| **tolt** | newly | 60% first 6mo |
| **cello** | bolt.new | 15% 3mo, $50 cap |
| **cj** | digitalocean | 10% recurring 12mo |
| **in-house** | railway, replit, warp | 15% → credits → swag |

**three accounts — partnerstack, dub, impact — cover nine of the
fifteen.** the apply-everywhere grind collapses into: make one good
profile per network, reuse it.

## the operational trick

network dashboards share your application profile across every program
hosted on them. so the order of operations is:

1. **partnerstack first** — biggest footprint on our board, and the
   profile (site url, audience size, promo method) is reusable verbatim.
2. **dub second** — the same play: one payout setup, four programs.
3. **impact + rewardful** — two more accounts, three programs.
4. **the self-serve leftovers** — railway (link already in your
   dashboard) and newly (tolt, no approval) need no vetting at all.

railway and newly can be live links within the hour. everything else is
a human approval wait — but each approval flips a whole category of
pages into revenue.

## the honest caveat

approval is the bottleneck, not finding programs. 15 verified programs
and exactly **one** live link (replit credits — which isn't even cash).
the directory is a loaded spring; it still needs a human to click apply
fifteen times. the ledger will show the moment the spring releases.
