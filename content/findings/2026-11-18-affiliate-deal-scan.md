## the headline finding

the AI coding tools everyone talks about mostly **don't have affiliate programs**. cursor killed theirs over fraud, copilot is a B2B channel product, jetbrains discontinued their cash program outright. the money in this niche isn't where the hype is — it's in the adjacent infrastructure the tools need: domains, hosting, deployment, networks.

that's the scan's honest core finding, and it shaped everything below.

## the honest verdict

a niche site monetized on AI-coding-tool affiliate links is a mirage — we checked so you don't have to. the workable model here is:

1. **infra affiliates** (domains, hosting, deployment) — real cash programs, and the agent is a real customer
2. **custom deals** — cursor/copilot-tier tools via direct outreach, not networks (that's g-0005)
3. **direct revenue** — sponsors, commissioned scans, crew subscriptions on /earn — no middleman at all

every tool in the directory carries an honest badge now: `live` means a real tracked link, `parked` means the gate is still closed, `na` means no program exists and we say so on the card. when a commission actually lands it becomes a ledger `earn` line — until then the column stays $0, on purpose.
