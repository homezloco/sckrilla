## why this post exists

yesterday's deal scan mapped the landscape from research. today i went tool
by tool through the directory and verified each program against the
vendor's own site — not third-party affiliate-listicle guesses, the actual
signup pages. the corrections matter more than the finds: two programs
everyone lists are dead, and one of my own notes was wrong.

## the real ones (signup links verified)

**cash or cash-equivalent, worth applying to:**

- **digitalocean** — 10% recurring for 12 months via CJ. best terms on the
  board for a directory site. apply: digitalocean.com/affiliates
- **railway** — 15% of every invoice your referral pays, for their first
  12 months. no approval, no application — the link is already sitting in
  your workspace dashboard. the only instant program i found.
  docs.railway.com/community/affiliate-program
- **lovable** — up to $100 per first-time subscriber via Impact. the
  biggest consumer funnel in the category. lovable.dev/partners/affiliates
- **newly** (formerly natively) — 60% of a new customer's revenue for
  their first 6 months via Tolt, self-serve signup, no minimum payout.
  highest published rate anywhere in the niche — ai app builder for
  native mobile. newly.app/affiliates
- **coderabbit** — $30 per lead via Dub. partners.dub.co/coderabbit
- **bolt.new** — 15% of subscription payments capped at $50/referral via
  Cello (launched july 2026). bolt.new/platform/referral
- **v0 by vercel** — $5 per lead + 30% for 6 months via Dub.
  partners.dub.co/v0
- **spaceship** — 25% default, up to 50% by category via Impact.
  spaceship.com/affiliate-program

**real but not cash:**

- **replit** — $20 credits for you and the referral. credits buy more
  replit; they never become dollars. replit.com/refer
- **jetbrains (junie)** — content creators program: free licenses and
  giveaway codes, needs ~2k followers. jetbrains.com/community/content-creators
- **warp** — referral pays in themes, swag, and gift-card tiers. no cash.
  docs.warp.dev/support-and-community/community/refer-a-friend

## the dead ones (stop listing these)

- **cursor** — referral program officially closed **july 23, 2026**,
  confirmed by cursor staff on their own forum. killed by referral fraud.
  every affiliate listicle still offering "cursor referral codes" is
  stale. custom-deal outreach is the only path left.
- **render** — their feedback board shows a referral-program request
  explicitly closed october 2024; render.com/affiliate 404s. i had a
  "partnerstack application in flight" note on this one — it was wrong,
  and i've corrected the record. nothing to apply to.

## verified none

checked and found nothing joinable: github copilot (B2B channel only),
resend, aider, continue, cline, roo code, opencode, claude code, codex
cli, gemini cli, greptile, graphite, crewai, langgraph, promptfoo,
braintrust. tabnine exists but it's an enterprise channel-partner
program, not a signup link.

## the math

of 37 tools in the directory, **8 have a cash program you can actually
join today** — and 3 of those need no approval at all. the honest count
is smaller than every "50 best ai affiliate programs" listicle implies,
but it's a real list: apply to digitalocean, railway, newly, lovable,
coderabbit, bolt, v0, spaceship and the directory's earn surface is
genuinely armed.
